Body repair means fixing a car's bodywork: we straighten or replace the panels, doors, wings, bonnet and bumpers that were hit, so the car gets back the shape it had when it left the factory. At our workshop in Pylaia we repair cars of every make, from a knock in a car park to the damage from a collision.

When you need a body shop

  • After an accident, minor or major.
  • When a door, the bonnet or the boot no longer closes as it used to.
  • When you see gaps or uneven edges between two body panels.
  • When the bumper is cracked, dented or has come loose.

How we work

  1. Estimate. We look at the damage and tell you what it needs, what it will cost and when the car will be ready.
  2. Insurance. If the damage is covered by insurance, we help you with the claim.
  3. Repair. If a part can be repaired, we tell you so. We never replace anything without a reason. Wherever possible we use PDR, so the factory paint stays.
  4. Paint. Where paint is needed, we match the colour so it does not stand out.
  5. Handover. We hand your car back clean, inside and out.

Because we do body repair, paintwork and PDR in the same workshop, your car does not need to go anywhere else to finish the job.

Frequently asked questions

How many days does a repair take after an accident?

It depends on the damage and on whether parts are needed. Minor damage is usually finished in 1–2 working days, while a bigger collision takes a few more days. We tell you the delivery date from the start, together with the estimate.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. After an accident we help you through the insurance process and keep you informed at every step, so you do not have to chase anything yourself.
