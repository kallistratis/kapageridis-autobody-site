PDR: paintless dent repair

For dents where the paint is not broken: no filler, no paint, and the car keeps its factory finish.

PDR (Paintless Dent Repair) is a way of repairing dents with no filler and no paint. Using special tools we push the metal from behind and bring it back, little by little, to its original shape. The car's factory paint stays untouched.

When PDR works

  • Door dings from other cars in car parks.
  • Hail damage.
  • Small dents in a door, wing, bonnet or roof, where the paint is not broken.

When it does not work: when the paint has cracked or the metal has stretched too much, for example a deep dent on an edge. Then the panel needs body repair and paint. We tell you from the start, and because we do both, the repair carries on in the same workshop.

Why choose PDR

  • The factory paint stays, which also matters when you sell the car.
  • It is usually faster than body repair and paint.
  • It is usually cheaper, because no paint materials are needed.

How it is done

  1. Assessment: we examine the dent under a special light to see its shape and depth.
  2. Access: we reach the back of the panel, removing trim or lights for a while where needed.
  3. Repair: with special tools we push the metal gradually, at many small points, until it is straight.
  4. Check: we inspect the surface under the light again, so that no trace is left.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDR a permanent repair?

Yes. The metal returns to its original shape and the dent does not come back. Because no filler or new paint is used, there is nothing that could crack or fade later.

How do I find out if my dent can be fixed with PDR?

Book an appointment with the “Book an appointment” form or call us. Bring the car in, we examine the dent under a special light and tell you on the spot whether PDR will work.
