Privacy policy

Which of your details we use, why, and which cookies this site uses.

Who we are

The business responsible for your details is Καπαγερίδης Ιωάννης – Φανοποιείο · Βαφείο · PDR (Kapageridis Auto Body), Steliou Kazantzidi 30, Pylaia 555 35, Thessaloniki, Greece. For anything about your details, contact us on +30 2310 471606 or at kapageridis.gr@yahoo.com.

When you contact us

When you call or email us, we use what you give us (name, phone number, email, photos of the damage, details of the car) only to answer you, to give you an estimate and to carry out the repair. We do not sell it or pass it on to anyone else, unless it is needed for your insurance claim with your agreement or the law requires it (for example for issuing invoices).

Emails pass through our email provider, under its own terms.

Google Maps

The home page shows a map from Google Maps, so you can find the workshop easily. When you scroll near the map, your browser loads it directly from Google. Google then receives your IP address and may use its own cookies; Google's privacy policy applies to these. If you do not want the map to load, do not scroll to the end of the home page.

Appointment form

What you fill in on the “Book an appointment” page (car, what happened, insurance, day and time, full name, mobile number) is stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in our Google account and sent to us automatically, through the automation service Make, as a Telegram message, so that we can confirm the appointment with a text message to your mobile.

We use these details only for the appointment, the estimate and, if you give us the job, the repair, and we keep them only as long as needed for that. Google and Telegram may process data outside the EU. You can ask us at any time to delete your request.

What is stored on your device

The site itself sets no cookies. The fonts load from this site itself, not from Google.

Hosting

Like every website, the server that hosts these pages records technical details of visits (IP address, time, page, browser) for security and to keep the site running.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, to restrict or stop their use, and to receive them in electronic form. Contact us on +30 2310 471606 or at kapageridis.gr@yahoo.com. If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.
